# `decisions/` — daily journal of what was seen, decided, executed

One folder per trading day. Three files per folder.

## Files

**`morning_brief.md`** — your pre-market read. What did the alignment look like?
Which Hedgeye signals + SpotGamma levels were saying what? What's the plan
going into the session?

**`trades.md`** — what got executed in Fidelity Mobile. Ticker, action,
size in $ and bps, account, the WHY in one sentence.

**`observations.md`** — anything notable. A pattern that surprised you, an
outcome that contradicted the read, a tool quirk to remember, a thought to
revisit on the weekend. Free-form.

## Style

Brief. One paragraph or a few bullet lines per item. The point is capturing
the THINKING in the moment so weekly reviews can compare reasoning to outcomes.
Long entries are allowed but not required.

## How to write it

Easiest: tell me about your day from Dispatch and I'll write the entry into
the right file. "Bought 50bps OIH at 432, here's why..." → I capture it as a
trades.md entry. You don't have to do file editing yourself.
